Makeup Artistry Training Agreement

1. Training Scope & Expectations

The training session is an intensive 7-working-days program. Time duration 2 hours per day The Trainee will receive hands-on instruction in skin preparation, color theory, foundation blending and highlighting/contouring.

2. Logistics: Models & Products

  • Products: High-end makeup products and tools will be provided for use on-site only during training hours.(Optional)
  • Models: Live models will be provided for demonstration. Trainees may bring a personal model for practice sessions with 24-hour prior notice to the Trainer.

3. Attendance & Punctuality

Trainees must be available and on time for all sessions. Training starts promptly at the scheduled time. Missed hours will be rescheduled.

4. Payment Terms

The total cost of the training is 1,000 GHS. 50% payment must be completed before the commencement of the first session. All payments are non-refundable.
